C-04
REFERENCE: A presidential decree of October 2017 ordered the change for 2025; in January 2021 the target was moved to 2031.

LLM only:
Kazakhstan formally decided to move Kazakh from Cyrillic to a Latin-based alphabet in **2017**:

- **12 April 2017:** President Nursultan Nazarbayev announced and ordered preparations for the change.
- **27 October 2017:** A presidential decree formally approved the Latin-script transition.

The original target was **2025**, but the timetable was later extended. The transition is now generally scheduled to be completed by **2031**.

BM25 RAG:
Kazakhstan decided to switch from Cyrillic to Latin in **October 2017**, by presidential decree. The completion deadline was later postponed from **2025 to 2031**.

Embedding RAG:

REFERENCE: The corpus does not say. GO-20 says master's students have a separate scheme that the handbook does not describe.

LLM only:
It depends on your country, school, program, enrollment, and financial circumstances. If you mean the **U.S.**, master’s students generally **cannot receive the federal Pell Grant or FSEOG**, which are mainly for undergraduates. Need-based funding may instead come from:

- **University or department grants**—amounts vary widely and may cover part or all of tuition.
- **State grants**—eligibility depends on the state and program.
- **Federal Work-Study**—if your school participates and you demonstrate need.
- **Institutional fellowships or assistantships**—often include tuition remission and/or a stipend.
- **Private or professional-organization grants**.

Your FAFSA can still determine eligibility for some institutional aid, work-study, and federal loans, but it does not produce one universal “master’s grant amount.” To estimate your potential aid, I’d need your **country, state/province, school, program, enrollment status, and approximate household income/assets**.
